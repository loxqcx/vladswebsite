How we partner

A few ways to work together.

Most creators who reach out already have a game with real players — or an idea worth building. Here's how it typically goes.

Sell your game

Built something with real players? We'll buy it outright, price it against your actual stats, and take over operations after close.

Build with us

Have an idea but not the team? We can build it from prototype to launch, handling production, art, and systems along the way.

Take investment

Not ready to sell? We invest and stay involved — you keep building while we help grow the player base.

Clean handoff

Group access, assets, player communications, and the first stretch of live-ops — we manage the transition end to end.
